# Customer invoice center

The console provides application, optional fee payment, and private delivery. Ordinary and special invoices require a company name and tax ID. Older personal applications and issued documents remain accessible, but a pending personal application cannot proceed to issuance. Finance issues the invoice externally and records the resulting PDF and number. This feature does not issue tax invoices, send email, or initiate an external refund. Before enabling new applications, finance must approve the issuing entity, eligible categories, fees, tax settings and operating procedure.

## Rollout

- The customer sidebar shows Invoice Center by default. Without a verified `CUSTOMER_INVOICE_EPAY_MERCHANT_SCOPE`, it is read-only and cannot accept new applications. After finance approval, set the verified scope of the single issuing Epay entity (the receipt merchant ID, not a payment secret). An existing explicit `CUSTOMER_INVOICE_ENABLED=false` still hides the sidebar entry and pauses new intake; remove it or set it to true to use the default. Configure identically across nodes and restart after changes (recreate containers if their environment changed).
- Public `/api/status` separates `customer_invoice_center_visible` (sidebar visibility) from `customer_invoice_enabled` (the master switch and merchant scope permit intake). It does not expose the merchant ID. Per-type and category settings still apply.
- Eligible self-service candidates are the current customer's successful non-agent top-ups backed by matching verified live Epay CNY receipts within the last 30 days (based on payment time). A request can contain at most 100 whole orders, with no double reservation across pending or issued applications. Invoice amount is verified cash principal in cents, not wallet credit after discounts or bonuses.
- Historical orders without matching receipts, unsupported payment instruments, redemptions without a customer payment record tied to this merchant, credits with no cash payment, and unknown or inconsistent payments do not qualify automatically. Direct edge cases to the existing support process; do not fabricate historical receipts.
- In **Invoice management → Invoice settings**, a root administrator can independently enable ordinary and special invoice applications, enable the fee for each type, and set its fee rate and configured tax rate. Both types start enabled under the global switch, and fees are off. The server freezes the applied settings on each new application. It calculates the fee from the sum of eligible cash principal, rounded once to the nearest cent, half up. The separately recorded configured tax rate is not part of that calculation, does not charge a second tax, and does not alter the invoice principal or the issued PDF.
- **Invoice categories** in the same settings allow a root administrator to add, rename, enable or disable customer choices. The existing Technical services and Software development categories remain enabled by default. The list applies to both invoice types. Only enabled categories may be selected for new applications; disabling them all pauses new applications without changing history, payment or review. Existing applications keep their original category label. Choose labels approved by finance; a custom console label does not set an external tax classification or change an issued PDF. Wait until all serving instances run the category-aware build before editing these settings; older builds do not enforce the custom list.
- When a positive fee is due, the customer must explicitly choose a supported method and confirm the payment. The application is not actionable for issuance until payment is confirmed. Eligible Epay sub-methods use a separate fixed-amount fee order under the same configured merchant; only a verified provider callback can mark it paid. Returning from the payment page is not proof of settlement. Fee payments never top up a wallet, earn bonuses, or become eligible principal for another invoice.
- Optional paid-wallet fee payment is disabled unless payment compliance is enabled and `CUSTOMER_INVOICE_WALLET_QUOTA_PER_CNY_CENT` is set to a valid, explicit integer conversion across all nodes. The customer sees the frozen fee in cents and debit in paid quota units. Reward and promotional balances are excluded. An eligible cancellation or rejection reverses the exact original paid-quota debit; it is not a cash refund. Do not infer the conversion from a display exchange rate or top-up pricing.
- Once an Epay checkout starts, the hold cannot be released or paid by a different method while the transaction may still settle. Resume the same payment or reconcile it with finance. An externally paid fee on an application later cancelled or rejected is put in fee-refund review. Verify and handle any actual refund with the approved merchant process; never issue a wallet credit in place of an unverified cash fee refund.
- Reusing a new payment type has two independent requirements. A new Epay sub-method under the same merchant can use the existing verified-receipt and fixed-amount checkout adapters if enabled. A distinct payment gateway needs an explicitly supported invoice-eligible receipt scope and, separately, fee checkout/verification/reconciliation support before it can appear. Turning it on for recharge does not turn it on for invoices.
- The admin screen is for root staff only. Review the actual receipt, customer and any refunds against the merchant record first; the absence of a local refund event does not prove a payment is unrefunded. Reject unverifiable requests. Before touching the external issuance system, claim the application in the admin screen. The claim is durable and prevents owner cancellation or reuse of its orders while finance works. The claiming reviewer must verify the issuer, use the application ID as the external reconciliation reference, and record only the actual resulting invoice number and PDF (maximum 5 MiB).
- A claim never expires automatically. If issuance fails or the result is uncertain, reconcile it externally. The claiming reviewer may release the claim only after confirming no invoice was issued. This is not a void or reissue feature. In case of reviewer unavailability, keep the claim blocked until finance reconciles it; do not directly clear the database claim. The customer's email is contact information; this feature does not email a document.
- For a refund discovered after issuance, flag the existing record for financial reconciliation. Do not release the original purchase or overwrite its issued document. Handle any external correction, voiding or replacement through the approved finance process.
- Keep database access, backups and replicas restricted and encrypted at rest. Invoice application details can include tax and bank details. Do not log, export, forward or store them in public attachment storage. The authenticated download is an attachment with no cache; no public URL is issued.

## Acceptance and rollback

Check fresh and upgraded PostgreSQL 18.6 installations and restart twice after migration. Exercise the feature with synthetic receipts and PDF fixtures only: one-order and multi-order applications, independent settings and stale requests, company-only validation, concurrent duplicates, retry idempotency, non-owner access, invalid receipts, fee amount and provider binding, fee refund states, and terminal-state transitions. Check that free and externally paid applications do not change wallet balances, and that a wallet fee and reversal affect only the paid account with matching ledger entries. Check desktop and mobile console sizes and both feature-switch states. Test on a copy, never against the production database.

Set `CUSTOMER_INVOICE_ENABLED=false` on all nodes and restart to stop new applications. Existing history, private downloads, eligible cancellations, and payments on already accepted applications remain available through the direct feature route according to the current payment settings. Keep the original merchant and signing configuration until all already-started checkouts are reconciled, including after stopping intake. Once a fee checkout or debit exists, do not downgrade to a build that lacks its callback and reconciliation logic. Do not drop the invoice, fee settings, payment receipt, or ledger data as part of rollback.
